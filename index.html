A bit about me

I'm Alaa Aissa, 20 years old, originally from Algeria. I've been living in Rosenheim since December 2025, and I'm here to build something real — starting with my degree in Artificial Intelligence at TH Rosenheim.

I'd describe myself as disciplined, calm, and chill. I take my goals seriously — especially my studies and training — but I keep a balanced, simple lifestyle. Emotionally stable, respectful, and genuinely easy to be around.

I'm looking for a WG with good vibes and mutual respect — maybe the occasional shared meal or late-night chess game ♟️.

  • 🌅
    Early riser
    Consistent routine, early sleep. Mornings are my most productive time.
  • 🍳
    I cook regularly
    Mostly healthy meals — sometimes really good, sometimes just functional. Either way the kitchen stays spotless.
  • 🧹
    Very tidy
    I keep shared spaces clean and organized. It's just how I function best.
  • 🤫
    Quiet at home
    Quiet and respectful in shared environments. No chaos, no loud surprises at midnight.
  • 🌳
    Balanced social life
    I enjoy going out, eating out, parks — but I also love a calm evening in. Not a homebody, not a party animal.
  • 🐾
    No pets, no allergies
    Totally fine with flatmates who have pets.

Studying Artificial Intelligence at TH Rosenheim — a field I chose because I genuinely love it, not just because it's trending. Focused on software development, currently building with Django and React.

I also do a Minijob alongside my studies to stay independent. My days are structured: classes, gym, code, repeat.

Long-term goal: software developer or AI engineer. I'm playing the long game.

  • 🏋️
    The gym is a huge part of my life — I train consistently and take bodybuilding seriously
  • ♟️
    I play chess — it sharpens the mind the same way training sharpens the body
  • 💻
    Always have a side project going — currently building with Django & React
  • 🎵
    My playlist switches between Lana Del Rey at 2am and Benson Boone pre-workout
  • 📺
    Watched more series than I'd like to admit — Peaky Blinders and GoT are untouchable
  • 🌍
    Algerian roots, German ambitions. Here to grow.
  • ☕
    Black coffee, no sugar — just like my code
